    About Cosmoscope

    Planet and Moon positions are calculated for the exact moment you are watching. Stars are real: 109,000 of them from the HYG catalog, placed at their measured distances and drifting with their measured motions when you speed up time.

    Saved places, collections and shared links stay on your device. A shared link carries the list inside the link itself, and nothing is sent to a server.

    Trending (in the Atlas) counts visits anonymously: a place counts once a day per home or phone network when you fly there and stay 5 seconds ("Today" means the last 24 hours). No IP addresses, cookies or accounts are kept, tours don't count, and you can switch it off in Settings.

    Some things are drawn bigger or brighter than real life so you can see them. Those spots are always labeled at the bottom of the screen.

    Where the data comes from

    • Stars: HYG Database v4.1 by David Nash (CC BY-SA 4.0), built from Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese catalogs
    • Constellation lines and names: Stellarium modern sky culture (CC BY-SA 4.0)
    • Planet & Moon positions: Astronomy Engine by Don Cross (MIT), computed live
    • Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble & Black Marble. Moon imagery: NASA
    • Story facts: NASA, ESA, ESO, NASA Exoplanet Archive (sources on each card)
    • New worlds: NASA Exoplanet Archive, refreshed daily. Planets NASA confirmed in the last 90 days, placed at their measured distances
    • Milky Way, nearby galaxy shapes and the galaxy web: illustrations based on current maps
    • The faint background dust is an illustration, not real stars
